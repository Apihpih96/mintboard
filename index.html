Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. Every badge costs 0 — you only pay the network gas for your transaction. The contract refuses payments outright, and MintBoard never asks you to approve token spending.

Do I need an Agent ID?

Yes. Minting is gated to wallets holding an Agent ID, checked live against the registry on-chain. Agent IDs are claimed in the Binance App.

What happens if I miss a day?

Your streak restarts at 1 on your next mint — but every badge you already own stays yours forever. The daily countdown at the top shows exactly when the next day starts (UTC).

What are the badges worth?

They are free collectibles with no promised value or returns. The fun is the streak, the quests, the leaderboard — and the friends you bring along.

Is my wallet safe here?

MintBoard never asks for seed phrases, private keys, or passwords, never moves your funds, and never asks for token approvals. You review and confirm every transaction in your own wallet, and the contract source is public and verified.
